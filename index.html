How a visit goes

85°C
  1. 01

    Sauna

    15 minutes

    Cedar, lit from below. Sit high if you want it hotter. Leave when you’re glowing, not when you’re done.

  2. 02

    Plunge

    Two minutes, if you can

    Chest-deep, hands in. Breathe out longer than you breathe in. The first thirty seconds are the whole thing.

  3. 03

    Rest

    Ten minutes, or more

    Warm stones under your back, a tea you didn’t have to make, and nothing at all to do. Then again.

  4. Then again. Three rounds, or as many as you like. Book the circuit →

The space

Three rooms. One temperature at a time.

A 1920s bank building on Richmond Row, stripped to brick and cedar. The vault is the plunge.

Cedar · 85°

Sauna

Twelve seats on three tiers, a window onto the courtyard, and a stove we feed with birch. Quiet by house rule.

Stone · 8°

Plunge

Chilled, filtered, 1.2 metres deep, under a vaulted ceiling. There is a rail. Everybody uses it.

Warm stones · 21°

Rest Room

Heated stone loungers, a tea counter, blankets, and a strict no-phones rule we will remind you of exactly once.
